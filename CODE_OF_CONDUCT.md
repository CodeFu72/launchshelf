# Community conduct

LaunchShelf welcomes contributors of all experience levels. Be respectful, discuss
the work rather than attacking people, and help newcomers learn. Harassment,
discrimination, threats and sharing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and limit participation
when necessary. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content. A dedicated
private conduct contact has not been established; do not post personal details in
an issue.
